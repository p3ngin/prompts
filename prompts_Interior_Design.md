# Interior Design AI Prompts

## Interior Design Planning

These prompts have the AI act as an interior design professional to help plan improvements to a single room or a whole home. They cover full room redesigns at different budgets, furniture layout, color schemes, layered lighting, adapting a room to a specific style, and a prioritized 12-month renovation plan. Replace the `[BRACKETED]` placeholders with your own details before using a prompt; options inside a bracket are separated by `/`.

### 1. Room Redesign Prompt

```text
Act as a professional interior designer.

I am uploading a photo of my [ROOM TYPE: living room / office / bedroom / kitchen / home office].

Analyze the current space and redesign it completely.
Keep the same basic structure, but reimagine the furniture arrangement, color palette, lighting, and decor.

Give me three design directions:
- One under $500 to implement
- One mid-range up to $2,000
- One premium

For each, describe the four most impactful changes to make first and why they work in this specific space.
```

### 2. Shopping List Prompt

```text
Act as a space planner.

My [ROOM TYPE] is [LENGTH] by [WIDTH] feet and I currently have these pieces of furniture: [LIST EACH ITEM].

The main problem with the current arrangement is [SPECIFIC PROBLEM: the room feels cramped / there is no clear focal point / traffic flow is bad / the space feels too empty].

Give me three different arrangements that solve this problem.
For each one, draw a simple text diagram and explain why this layout works better than what I have now.
```

### 3. Color Consultant Prompt

```text
Act as an interior color consultant.

My [ROOM TYPE] currently has [CURRENT WALL COLOR] walls and [MAIN FURNITURE COLOR/MATERIAL].
Natural light comes from the [DIRECTION: north / south / east / west].

Tell me the single biggest color mistake I am making.

Then give me a complete new color scheme:
- Main wall color
- Accent wall color
- Trim color
- Two furniture accent colors

Include the exact paint brand and color name for each.
```

### 5. Lighting Design Prompt

```text
Act as a residential lighting designer.

My [ROOM TYPE] currently has [DESCRIBE EXISTING LIGHTING: one overhead fixture / recessed cans / ceiling fan with light].
It feels [TOO DARK / TOO HARSH / FLAT AND UNINVITING].

Design a complete layered lighting plan:
- Ambient lighting for general illumination
- Task lighting for functional areas
- Accent lighting for depth

For each layer, give me:
- The product type
- Ideal placement
- Recommended bulb temperature in Kelvin
- One specific product name to look up
```

### 6. Style Translation Prompt

```text
Act as an interior designer specializing in [TARGET STYLE: Japandi / mid-century modern / coastal / industrial / maximalist / cottagecore].

I want to transform my [ROOM TYPE] into this aesthetic without it looking like a catalog page.

Give me 10 specific changes I can make, ordered from cheapest and easiest to most involved.

For each change, name:
- The exact item or action
- Its approximate cost
- The one visual impact it creates in this specific room
```

### 7. Whole Home Plan Prompt

```text
Act as a professional interior designer managing a full renovation.

My home has [NUMBER] rooms and a total budget of $[AMOUNT].
The rooms are: [LIST ALL ROOMS].

Prioritize which rooms to improve first for the biggest impact on daily life.
Then build a quarterly plan for the next 12 months.

For my top-priority room, give me a complete starter action list:
- Three things to buy
- Two things to remove
- One thing to reposition this weekend for an immediate upgrade
```
